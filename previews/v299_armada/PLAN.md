# The Vorn Armada: the Vorn's hard-mode dungeon (plan)

**The boss:** Admiral Vexis, Voice of the Hive. **The place:** the Vorn hive-fleet, circling a red star in the outer End.

This is meant to be the second-hardest dungeon in the game: below the Undercrown, above everything else.

## Lore
The Vorn are a hive, and every ship and every trooper answers one voice. The fleet's heart is **the Hive-Star**, a dying red giant. The Armada circles it and drinks it: **red Xenite** is condensed star-mass, which is why it grows only aboard Dreadnoughts.

The voice of the hive is **Admiral Vexis**. Vexis was once a single Vorn, one of the first to be "improved". Every invasion night is Vexis reaching out across the stars toward your world.

## Finding it (hard mode only)
1. **Sabotage a Dreadnought's reactor in hard mode.** Among the vault's spoils are **the Hive-Star Coordinates**, a star-chart the hive never meant to lose.
2. **Go to the End** (anywhere past the main island) and use the coordinates. The signal goes out, and a **tractor beam** answers.
3. **The arrival:** a cinematic of about 10 s. You are lifted into the dark, the star blooms into view with the fleet black against it, and the title reads *THE VORN ARMADA*. You are set down on the Dock.
4. A beam-pad on the Dock takes you back where you came from.

## The fleet (one per world, at a fixed far spot in the End, about 240 x 96 x 240)
**The Hive-Star sits at the centre:** a burning sphere about 45 across, with flares and a corona. It is lethal to touch, and the space around it scorches you.

**Seven ships orbit it**, joined by **hard-light bridges** that switch on as you solve your way through. Fall into the void and the fleet's tractor beam catches you: you take a hard hit and are set back on the last bridge-pad you crossed. Nothing is lost to the void.

1. **The Dock** (the arrival frigate). The hub, with the Fleet Map hologram and three locked bridges. ◆ A *gravity-lift* puzzle opens the first.
2. **The Brood-Carrier** (bio-labs, green light, specimen tanks):
   - ◆ *Tank sequence*: the specimen tanks pulse in an order, and you repeat it.
   - ⚔ A bio-wave of bioengineered slimes, bio phantoms and troopers.
   - ◆ *Containment*: lights-out on the lab's quarantine grid.
   - ☠ **The Brood-Warden**, a hulking bio-mutant mini-boss, which drops **Cipher I**.
3. **The Star-Forge** (the Xenite foundry, molten red light):
   - ◆ *Power routing*: switch conduits until all four reactors read green.
   - ⚔ Forge drones.
   - ◆ *Smelt order*: offer the right Xenite in the right sockets.
   - ☠ **The Forgemaster**, the Warlord's elder, which drops **Cipher II**.
4. **The Navigator** (an observatory ship in low gravity):
   - ◆ *Star-chart*: shoot the constellations in the order the chart shows.
   - ◆ *Zero-G crossing*: drift between floating platforms with the gravity lifts.
   - ⚔ Saucer squadrons.
   - It drops **Cipher III**.
5. & 6. **The Twin Dreadnoughts** (warships, combat-heavy):
   - Their reactors are guarded by Vorn Guardians and turret nests.
   - ⚔ Sabotage each reactor under fire (four conduits, plus waves).
   - Each opens an armoury.
   - **These are the dreadnoughts you attack again later to bring the invasions back.**
7. **The Flagship, the Hive-Throne:**
   - **The Command Lock:** set the three Ciphers.
   - ⚔ **The Gauntlet:** a long boarding corridor swept by sentry lasers. Move between the sweeps, and fight what the alarms call in.
   - **The Bridge:** the arena. A round hall about 64 across, whose far wall is a vast window filling with the red star. Vexis waits at the helm, inside its warframe.

**Hidden rooms**, each holding a unique relic. Some are opened by the followers who'd know the way:
- **Zib's Lost Squad**, opened by Zib, who knows the Donadian door-code.
- **Specimen Kennel**, where Donado sniffs out a vent.
- **The Mainframe Core**, which the Cursor hacks open.
- **The Hull Garden**, reached by the Sky Buddies flying out to an outside ledge.
- **Two classic secret rooms.**
- **The Hive Vault**, behind the helm, which opens after the fall.

## The fight: Admiral Vexis, in three phases
Every phase has its own entrance, music and title, with everyone's camera held on Vexis.

- **The coming:** you cross the bridge and the helm turns. Vexis rises in the colossal warframe, about 12 blocks tall. Its systems light up section by section, the window floods red, and the hive speaks through it.
- **I. THE WARFRAME:**
  - Its attacks are plasma-cannon sweeps, missile barrages (dodge the marked circles), stomp shockwaves, and drop-pods of troopers.
  - **Four shield pylons** make it near-invulnerable. Overload each one by hitting it while it's charged, then strike the frame.
- **II. THE HIVE-LINK:**
  - The warframe is torn open, and Vexis climbs out: a towering bioengineered Vorn. It takes to the air on gravity.
  - Its attacks are gravity wells that drag you in, *improvement beams* (being "improved" slows and weakens you; break line of sight), and hologram clones (find the real one).
  - It also calls drone swarms.
- **III. STARFIRE:**
  - Vexis taps the star itself. The great window opens and the bridge becomes a furnace.
  - **Star-flares** sweep the deck (shelter behind the shield pylons' husks), and gravity flips in zones.
  - It fires a solar lance, and at the end it frenzies.
- **The fall (cinematic):** the hive-link is severed, and the hive's voice goes silent for the first time in an age. Vexis, alone in its own head, kneels: *"...one. I am only one."*
  - **The invasion nights end.** The fleet turns away from your world.
  - Vexis joins you as a follower.

## After
- **Invasion nights end** when Vexis falls.
- **Bringing them back:** **attack a Dreadnought again**, whether one of the Armada's twin warships or one over your world, by triggering its alarm or sabotaging its reactor. *"The hive remembers this world."* That re-arms the invasions **and** lets you fight Vexis again.
- **Vexis, the Unlinked, as a follower:** in its small warframe, with the full checklist (menu, frame trick, figurine, Cecil doll, sitting, cosmetics, rare aura, Partner Wheel, shiny, duos with Bender, Capshun, Henry and Nia). Its powers are a plasma cannon, a gravity well, deploying a drone, and a deflector. Its rare unlock is a **full-size warframe form**, like the Sovereign's Unbound one.
- **Top-tier alien gear, the Starforged set**, above the Warplate:
  - **Starforged Exoplate**, a four-piece armour set. Its set bonus is hover-jets (a slowed fall and a burst jump) and a star-shield that soaks a blow.
  - **The Starfire Lance**, a melee weapon whose charged swing fires a beam.
  - **The Singularity Launcher**, which fires gravity bombs that pull mobs together.
  - **The Hive-Breaker**, a heavy axe.
  - **The Admiral's Crown.**
  - **Star-Cores:** they drop from the Ciphers' guardians and from Vexis. Spend them, with red Xenite, at a new Starforge bench aboard the Dock.
- **The dungeon resets** about three minutes after the fall, as the Undercrown does.

## Music
- **Exploration:** tiers for the Armada (synth arpeggios, theremin, brass, a choir of the hive) that build as Ciphers are won.
- **Fights:** separate combat tracks.
- **The boss:** an intro, three phases with turn stings, a finale, and a music disc.
